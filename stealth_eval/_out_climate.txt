Climate change. It's a big one for us now. Greenhouse gases are climbing. This shifts global weather. Disasters are hitting harder, more often. These changes affect everything: ecosystems, farms, people everywhere. Coastal towns face rising seas. Long droughts hurt food supplies in tough spots. We all need to team up. Governments, companies, folks like us. We must push for lasting fixes. Move toward an economy with less carbon. What we do now matters hugely. It sets up the planet for kids and grandkids.
